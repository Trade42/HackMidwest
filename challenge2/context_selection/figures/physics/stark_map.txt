Caesium energy levels near 60S (blue) against a DC electric field, computed with ARC by diagonalising 912 states (n = 54 to 61, all l, |mj| = 1/2). Left: the low-l states (60S, 59P, 60P, 58D, 59D) shift quadratically, the high-l manifold fans out linearly. Right: the 60S shift at small fields with the quadratic law fitted below 0.1 V/cm, giving a scalar polarizability of 197.1 MHz cm^2/V^2, so a field of 0.1 V/cm shifts the level by -0.99 MHz. The 60S shift stays within 5% of the quadratic law up to 0.68 V/cm (shaded), where avoided crossings with the manifold start to mix the state. It first meets the fan of the n = 56 manifold near 0.32 V/cm, in crossings too narrow to matter at that point. The level n = 60 is illustrative: we do not know which levels Sqale uses, and the field values are not Sqale numbers. A shift of the Rydberg level during a gate is a phase error on the CZ.
