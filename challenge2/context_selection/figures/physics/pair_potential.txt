Interaction between a caesium atom and a rubidium-87 atom, both in 60S1/2 (illustrative levels, m = 1/2 each, interatomic axis along the quantisation axis), computed with ARC: full diagonalisation of 561 pair states (blue shows the share of the original pair state) with the perturbative van der Waals curve on top. C6 = -205.4 GHz um^6 with V = -C6/R^6, so the interaction is repulsive. At the cited 5.5 um Rb-Cs spacing the van der Waals shift is 7.4 MHz; at twice that distance it is 0.12 MHz, 64 times weaker. The diagonalised shift at 5.5 um is 7.5 MHz and it crosses the assumed drive at 7.7 um. For an ASSUMED laser drive of 1 MHz (Rabi frequency over 2 pi) the blockade radius, where the interaction equals the drive, is 7.7 um; for an assumed 5 MHz it is 5.9 um. The Le Roy radius, below which the calculation is not valid, is 1.07 um. The drive is an assumption and the levels are illustrative: neither is a Sqale number. Only close neighbours blockade each other, so a qubit that must interact with every data qubit needs routing on a grid.
