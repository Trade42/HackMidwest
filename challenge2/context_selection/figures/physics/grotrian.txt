Energy levels of caesium and rubidium-87 computed with ARC 3.10.2. Arrows mark the two-photon path to a Rydberg state. Caesium: 6S1/2 to 7P1/2 at 459.4 nm, then to 60S1/2 at 1041.0 nm. Rubidium-87: 5S1/2 to 6P1/2 at 421.7 nm, then to 60S1/2 at 1005.9 nm. The Rydberg level n = 60 is illustrative: we do not know which levels Sqale uses, and the second wavelength shifts by a few nm with n. During a CZ only the qubit's |1> state is excited along this path.
